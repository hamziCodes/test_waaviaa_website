Color

Warm cream. Warm dark. Never a void, never a glare.

Both modes share the same warm undertone instead of running cool-gray in one and cool-black in the other. Text mirrors the pairing: cream text sits on the dark surface, ink text sits on the cream surface — same two colors, swapped roles.

DEPTH & TEXTURE — implementation spec

Grain: a fixed, full-viewport SVG fractalNoise layer, mix-blend-mode: overlay, opacity 0.035 (light) / 0.05 (dark), tiled at 180px. Applied once, globally — never regenerated per component.

Ambient glow: one soft radial gradient in the accent color, ~8% opacity (light) / ~16% opacity (dark), positioned above the hero only. This is the one place the brand spends a gradient — it never repeats lower on the page.

Card depth: earned only on hover (a soft accent-tinted shadow), not applied statically to every card at rest — a shadow under everything is the one thing that makes a page look templated.

Spacing

4px grid, no exceptions

Every margin, padding, and gap on the site is one of these values. No arbitrary numbers like 15px or 22px — this alone is why the layout feels engineered rather than eyeballed.

Guardrails

Keep it out of AI-slop territory

The fastest way to lose the brand is to let a builder tool apply its own defaults. These are the defaults to block, every time.

Do

  • Warm cream / warm dark, both grained, both mirrored in text color
  • One soft ambient glow, hero only — never repeated as a page-wide wash
  • Flat surfaces, 1px borders, 8–12px radius, shadow earned on hover only
  • Real numbers instead of adjectives
  • The waveform + animated wordmark as the one loud, alive element

Don't

  • Pure white glare or a flat black void
  • Purple-to-blue gradients, glassmorphism, blurred blobs
  • Full-pill buttons, or a shadow under every single card
  • Sparkle icons, stock headset photography, robot heads
  • Two different visual systems for light vs. dark
