A2 · Raven Dial

Same swatches: Raven #0A0A0C, Ice #CFE6F5, Poppy #E8352B.

  • Type: Unbounded 700, tight tracking, sentence case; “listen” as an ice outline. Labels in Space Mono.
  • Device: a flat poppy disc cropped off the corner, like a signal dial, over a 90px ice grid at 14% opacity.
  • Flat colour, no glow; the black reads as a technical instrument.

B2 · Sidetone Chat

Same swatches: Midnight #002941, Soft blue #B6DFED, Coral #FD6E6E.

  • Type: Syne 800, lowercase wordmark, −3% tracking.
  • Device: the headline set across three speech bubbles (filled blue, filled coral, outlined), ending with a typing-dots bubble.
  • Navy ground inverted from round 1; the conversation motif makes the voice idea literal.

C2 · Ripple Paper

Same swatches: Aubergine #1A0B2E, Lime #C8F169, Lilac #B8A4FF, Bone #F4EFE6.

  • Type: Instrument Serif, large with italic wordmark; “listen” highlighted in lime. Labels in Space Mono.
  • Device: a solid lilac disc with two aubergine hairline rings, a tilted lime sticker, and magazine rules top and bottom.
  • Bone paper ground gives a printed, issue-style feel that suits a series of numbered sections.

Which I’d carry forward: C, Ripple

A is the most striking single image, but black-with-red-light is the default look for AI tooling and it would blur into the feed. B has great momentum, but its athletic energy suits sport more than speech. C is the only one that visualises what WAVIA does (a voice radiating outward), it reads clearly at thumbnail size, and the cream Studio paper UI (#FFF6E5) sits naturally on aubergine, so the Studio carousels will pop. If you want a safer, more premium-tech mood, A is the fallback; its red light also gives you a strong reel-cover device.
